# Privacy notice - SpinLab DJ

**English** · [Italiano](PRIVACY.md)

> Text to be reviewed by a lawyer or privacy consultant before publication. Last revision: 26 November 2026, based on the
> behaviour of version 0.2.0. If this translation and the Italian text differ, the Italian text prevails.

## In short

- **SpinLab does not collect personal data** and has no account: there is no registration, usage statistics, advertising or behaviour
  analysis.
- **Your tracks, your library, your settings and your recordings stay on your computer** and are not sent to the author or to
  anyone else.
- **The Program does not connect to the internet** during normal use: the installer already contains everything it needs. A connection
  happens only if the model files (stems or beats) are missing, in which case they are downloaded from their public addresses (Hugging Face, GitHub);
  with the official installer this does not happen.

## Donations

The «Dona con PayPal» (Donate with PayPal) button opens the PayPal website in your browser. The donation takes place entirely on PayPal, which processes your data
as an independent controller according to its own notice (https://www.paypal.com/privacy). The author receives from PayPal only the information that
PayPal passes on to the recipient of a payment (for example name, email and amount) and uses it only to handle the donation and the
legal obligations.
